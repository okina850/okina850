## Summary

I studied physics at Osaka University and am now a master's student at Tokyo Metropolitan University, where I study **applied mathematics with a focus on financial engineering**.

My research ranges from **random number generation**, a fundamental technology for financial simulation, to machine learning, including **work on improving large language models**.

I also studied **gold's hedge capacity for an asset management research competition** organized by Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund (GPIF).

Outside of my studies, I did internships at **Japan Post Bank**, where I worked on **quantitative analysis for asset management and risk management** and analyzed a large amount of time series data.

**I'm someone who enjoys mathematics and science, with a passion for applying them to technology that supports people and society**.
